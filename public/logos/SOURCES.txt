Official vendor marks, downloaded from each vendor's own domain and committed
here so they are served same-origin and never hotlinked.

exa.svg         https://exa.ai/images/logo/exa-logo-blue.svg
apify.svg       https://apify.com/img/apify-logo/apify-logo-dark-512x141.svg
                (listed on https://apify.com/resources/brand)
cloudflare.svg  https://www.cloudflare.com/logo.svg

Downloaded 2026-08-17. Each file was checked to be valid XML containing no
<script> and no external references before being committed.

Not included, deliberately:

  Google Gemini, Google Maps
    Google does not publish these as freely downloadable SVGs, and its brand
    guidelines do not permit recoloring or otherwise modifying its marks, which
    is exactly what the monochrome treatment used in this strip does. Those rows
    stay as styled text rather than shipping a modified Google mark or an
    unofficial redraw.

  Cloudflare press kit
    The official press-kit archive linked from cloudflare.com/press-kit contains
    only JPG and PNG, no SVG. The file above is the mark Cloudflare serves from
    its own root domain, and it carries a <title>Cloudflare logo</title>.

  DataSF
    No strong logo mark. Styled text by design.

  Upstash
    Not a dependency of this product. Resolved corners, generated imagery, the
    generation budget, and rate limiting all run on Cloudflare KV.
